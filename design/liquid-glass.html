Presence — one bead, four states

W Working…The bot is on a turn. Solid blue bead with a still inner mark.
B Needs your inputAn approval or a clarify question is waiting. Amber bead with a notch, pulsing slowly — the one presence state that moves.

The header subtitle mirrors the bead in words, so the state never depends on colour alone. The global connection state lives in the sidebar's gateway card, not on a bot. Motion is reserved for the one state that needs you: only the amber bead animates, and it holds still under reduced motion.
